How to Survive

  • Move: Use WASD or Arrow Keys. On mobile, use the joystick.
  • Combat: You auto-fire at the nearest enemy within range.
  • XP: Defeat enemies and collect glowing gems to level up.
  • Upgrades: Choose one of three random upgrades every time you level.
  • Pause: Press Escape or the pause button.
  • Win: Survive the full 60 seconds.
  • Lose: If your health reaches zero, the run ends.
  • Enemies: Chasers are balanced, Runners are fast, Brutes are heavy hitters.
